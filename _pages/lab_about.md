## Who We Are

The **Wicked Applied Systems Practices (WASP) Lab** is an academic research group dedicated to advancing the reliability, dependability, security, and resilience of emerging computing technologies.

Our research spans intelligent cybersecurity, trustworthy artificial intelligence, resilience engineering, and autonomous systems. We investigate why computing systems fail, how failures propagate, and how systems can be designed, evaluated, and improved to operate dependably under uncertainty.

WASP Lab brings together fundamental research, experimental systems engineering, and applied research to address complex challenges at the intersection of computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and technology-enabled operations.

---

## Our Mission

To advance **Reliable, Dependable, and Resilient (RDR) Computing** by understanding failure mechanisms and developing evidence-based engineering approaches that improve the security, trustworthiness, and resilience of intelligent computing systems.

## Our Vision

A future in which intelligent and interconnected computing systems can be trusted to operate dependably, adapt to uncertainty, and recover from unexpected failures.

As computing technologies become increasingly embedded in human and organizational activities, we aim to extend our research from technical system failures toward the broader challenges of sociotechnical resilience.

---

## Our Research Philosophy

Our research is guided by four principles:

**Failure-Driven Research.** We study failures not simply as undesirable outcomes, but as opportunities to understand system limitations and advance engineering knowledge.

**Evidence-Based Engineering.** We emphasize reproducible experiments, measurable outcomes, empirical validation, and rigorous evaluation.

**Research with Practical Relevance.** We investigate fundamental research questions while considering the challenges faced by real-world computing systems and operational environments.

**Interdisciplinary Collaboration.** We encourage collaboration across computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, engineering, and information technology management.

---

## Our Research Environment

WASP Lab provides a collaborative research environment where undergraduate students, graduate researchers, postdoctoral fellows, and academic and industry collaborators work together on challenging research problems.

We emphasize student development, scientific independence, knowledge sharing, responsible research practices, and opportunities to translate research outcomes into practical tools, datasets, and technologies.

Our research activities include experimental prototypes, cybersecurity testbeds, intelligent systems, and collaborative projects with academic and industry partners.


---

## Mentorship, Inclusion & Research Culture

At WASP Lab, we are committed to a **student-centred, inclusive, flexible, and collaborative research environment** that promotes academic excellence, professional development, trainee well-being, and equitable opportunities.

### Our Mentorship Philosophy

We view mentorship as a progressive process of developing independent, confident, and responsible researchers. Each trainee follows an **Individualized Training Plan (ITP)** tailored to their background, interests, and career aspirations.

Through regular individual meetings, collaborative discussions, peer mentorship, and progressively increasing responsibilities, we foster research independence, critical thinking, leadership, and professional skills.

We prioritize **research quality over publication prestige**, transparent authorship practices, work-life balance, and flexibility in accommodating individual circumstances.

### Equity, Diversity & Inclusion

We consider Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion (EDI) integral to research excellence and effective mentorship.

Our approach focuses on removing barriers to participation, fostering mutual respect and belonging, and ensuring equitable access to research opportunities, funding, internships, and professional development.

We actively encourage participation from underrepresented groups in computing and cybersecurity, support individualized accommodations and flexible working arrangements, and value the diverse perspectives that strengthen interdisciplinary research.

### Our Commitment

Our goal extends beyond producing research outcomes. We aim to develop **independent, ethical, resilient, and socially responsible professionals** equipped to succeed in academia, industry, and beyond.
