## Overview

This research investigates the reliability and resilience of
agentic AI systems operating in dynamic cybersecurity environments.

The project explores failure characterization, neuro-symbolic
guardrails, task-driven context enrichment, and systematic
evaluation of autonomous cybersecurity workflows.

## Research Directions

- Failure characterization and propagation in agentic systems
- Neuro-symbolic AI and knowledge-driven guardrails
- Reliable cybersecurity workflow orchestration
- Chaos engineering for agentic AI systems
